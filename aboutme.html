About Me

My Passion for Technology

Hello everyone! I'm Francesco Vito Giotta, a young man hailing from the province of Bari in Puglia, Italy. Born on August 14, 2006, my passion for technology has been with me since I was little. From exploits in flash games to hunting APKs, my journey started early.

A little hacker is born...

At 11, I was already reinstalling Windows 7 and hunting for product keys. Later, I discovered Kali Linux and Python, which ignited my interest in "micro-hacking" and the digital frontier.

Kali Linux: The Leap

Data Retrieval: Testing security via link-based data retrieval.
Dark Web: Exploring and understanding hidden networks safely.
OSINT: Gathering intelligence from open sources to find personal data and corporate .env files.

Family Tree Project

My OSINT skills led me to genealogy. I spent 2023 meticulously building my family tree using FamilySearch, uncovering migratory patterns and personal stories of my ancestors.

Trading and Investing

In 2024, I dove into finance, learning from mentors like Mr. RIP and Paolo Coletti. I enjoy the technical side: implementing algorithms and AI trading with TensorFlow.

Content Creation...

Since 2025, I've been sharing my knowledge on YouTube. Even if I'm a bit shy, the joy of helping others through technology makes it all worth it.
