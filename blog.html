Dumpster Diving #2: Fighting Xiaomi’s Bloatware

Hi everyone! In this second episode of ‘Dumpster Diving,’ I fought against Xiaomi’s bloatware...

How to Crack a Compressed File with John the Ripper

In this article I will explain how to crack a compressed file!

First of all you need to obtain two things:

  1. John-The-Ripper
  2. Password-Wordlist

Install John-The-Ripper:

sudo snap install john-the-ripper

You can create an encrypted .zip in Linux using:

zip archivename.zip filename1.txt filename2.txt -e

Then, hash the archive and run the bruteforce attack:

john-the-ripper.zip2john archive.zip > hash.txt

john-the-ripper hash.txt passwordlist.txt

USSD: The Secret Messages of Mobile Networks

Have you ever typed a cryptic string like *123# to check your prepaid balance? You were interacting with USSD (Unstructured Supplementary Service Data)—real-time sessions that travel where apps simply cannot reach.

Unlike SMS (store-and-forward), USSD creates an instantaneous dialogue directly between your device and the carrier's server.

Common USSD Codes:

  • *#06# — Displays your IMEI
  • *#67# — Checks call forwarding status
  • *#0*# — Opens hardware test menu (on many Androids)

DIY TouchPen for Capacitive Screens

Viniero: Educational Tokens on Solana

On August 4, 2025, some tokens named Viniero were created on the Solana blockchain for educational purposes. This project was born from a dual perspective on the cryptocurrency market. On one hand, there is a belief in the potential of this niche market. On the other hand, there is a fundamental question about the valuation of assets like Bitcoin, whose founder, the enigmatic Satoshi Nakamoto, remains anonymous after approximately 16 years. This fact raises questions about trust and decentralization.

In a purely educational and lighthearted spirit, the Viniero tokens were created. There are no current plans to list this token on any decentralized exchanges (DEXs) like Raydium, nor is an Initial DEX Offering (IDO) planned. The tokens’ name, Viniero, is a tribute to a girl surname that the creator, known as “LemonPower21”, met during a masterclass in Bari.

This project serves as a practical demonstration of the token creation process on the Solana blockchain, offering insights into the technical aspects of decentralized finance without any associated investment risk.

The vision for the devnet tokens has expanded to include an innovative application in the sustainable tourism sector. The core idea is to re-imagine tokens not as a financial asset, but as a digital souvenir.

On the devnet, a blockchain test environment, tokens can be created that are acquired at specific, real-world locations. These digital collectibles would serve as a unique commemorative souvenir, linking a travel experience to a digital entity. This approach maintains a focus on education and sustainability, providing a tangible, yet non-financial, connection to the places visited.

Steganography Basics

Steganography is a technique that allows us to hide a secret file (such as text) within an image or audio file. There are two common ways to achieve this:

  1. Modifying the Metadata: Inserting the data into the file’s header or descriptive tags.
  2. Changing the LSB (Least Significant Bit): Altering the lowest bit of the pixel or audio data itself to embed information without visibly changing the file.

In this video, I will show you how to create a steganographic image using the SteganoTool by FutureBoys.

ATTENTION: This tool allows you to encode ASCII messages directly into the file.

About Me & My Journey

Hello everyone, my name is Francesco Vito Giotta. I was born on August 14, 2006, in Putignano, Puglia. For several years now, I’ve been sharing parts of my code online, and today I want to share a bit more about the person behind the screen.

My fascination with computers started at the age of 7, when I first began “tinkering” with my parents’ desktop PC. As I grew older, this interest became a refuge. During middle school, I struggled to get along with my classmates and faced bullying; in that difficult time, the digital world offered me the tranquility that the physical world couldn’t.

In 2020, spending more time at home led me to discover Linux. I started with Linux Mint, then hopped through Kali, Ubuntu, and Arch. Now, in 2026, I have come full circle and am back to using Linux Mint. As they say, you never forget your first love.

However, this passion came with a physical cost. I spent far too much time sedentary, my only exercise being short walks in the woods with my dog. Eventually, my weight reached 120kg.

I hit my lowest point on March 11, 2023. While walking my dog—fittingly named Linux—he picked up a suspicious piece of meat on a bone. Despite my efforts to chase him down, I wasn’t fast enough to take it from him. When we got home, I found him foaming at the mouth; the bone had been poisoned.

Losing him that night was devastating. I was haunted by the guilt of not being fit or fast enough to save him from that “trap.”

Driven by that pain, I made several failed attempts to lose weight until October 2023. My family went to try the “Angel’s Flight” (Volo dell’Angelo) in Basilicata, but I was told the weight limit was 100kg. That was the final straw.

In November 2023, I started a disciplined journey, heavily inspired by David Goggins’ motivational videos. By July 2024, I had successfully lost 40kg.

In April 2024, I developed an interest in finance. By November, I was tracking all my finances through complex Excel sheets. This interest eventually led me to create “Petrolium,” a trading bot I worked on from August 2024 to January 2025. I archived the project in April 2025 after achieving my goal: writing a functional program useful for non-retail level trading.

I also learned a valuable lesson in content creation. After several failed YouTube attempts, I realized in April 2025 that the best strategy is simply to “create and not care.” I stopped deleting videos based on view counts and focused on the process instead.

Currently, I am completing my first year of Computer Engineering at the Politecnico di Torino. My goal is to graduate and eventually join a FAANG company (I still prefer the old acronym!).

Looking back, I hope to never encounter a day as dark as that March 11th again, but I carry the lessons of discipline and resilience with me every day. Thanks for your attention and for reading this far!
