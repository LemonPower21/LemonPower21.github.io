My Passion for Technology

Hello everyone! My name is Francesco Vito Giotta, born on August 14, 2006, from Putignano, in the province of Bari, Italy. My passion for computer science and technology has deep roots in my early childhood, driven from the very beginning by a strong curiosity for how software systems and digital architectures operate.

First Steps in Development & Hacking

By the age of 11, I was already configuring advanced operating systems and researching technical solutions for my problems. Later on, discovering Kali Linux and Python shaped my core interests toward cybersecurity and automation scripting (and even webscraping).

Areas of Focus and Competencies

Security Analysis: Evaluating and auditing link-based data retrieval and data protection protocols.
Network Security: Exploring hidden networks and robust infrastructure standards safely.
OSINT (Open Source Intelligence): Gathering and analyzing public intelligence sources for digital research and corporate threat prevention.

Genealogical Research Project

Applying OSINT research and analytical techniques, in 2023 I undertook a meticulous project to reconstruct my family tree using FamilySearch, uncovering migratory patterns and personal histories of my ancestors.

Finance and Investments

In 2024, I dove into financial markets, building analysis algorithms using Python libraries such as yfinance and pandas, while also evaluating AI-driven trading models with TensorFlow. Eventually, I realized that the most robust approach to trading is a long-term, fundamentals-oriented strategy.
